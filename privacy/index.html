Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to https://andever2026.github.io/Q-Card/.
Repo on GitHub: https://github.com/andever2026/Q-Card.

Your privacy is important. Here’s the short version of our policy:

  • When using this site as a guest, nothing gets stored to our database. The amount of data we collect in guest mode is the same as if the user downloads the page and uses it that way — that is, nothing is stored in our database in guest mode.
    This may change if we add ads in the future.
  • When you sign in with a Google account, we store the Google account name, email address, profile picture, IP address and some browser details. We also store your sets and some preferences in our Firebase database.
    More can be stored to our database, but we will ask for permissions before storing.
  • All personal information that we collect fulfills a purpose. Consenting to permissions works incrementally and is reversible.
  • You can delete your data and your account at any time in Settings.

Last updated: October 24, 2026.

Using the site as a guest

To keep everything functional locally, all your information and data are stored in your browser's storage.

This covers:

  • your vocab sets and kanji lists
  • your choices: picked sets, practice direction and mode, colors and light or dark
  • a practice you quit midway, including what you'd written for the question on screen, so Resume can bring it back
  • your practice history, if you turn on data collection in History (also stored locally)

Be careful with clearing cookies and website data, and backing up often (Settings → Export) is best practice. Your data is irrecoverable when lost.

You can delete this data at any time in Settings. Open Settings and choose Delete local storage.

Sign in with Google

By signing in with Google, you confirm that you are 13 or older.

Signing in for the first time creates a profile of you in Firebase. This includes sharing your name, email address, profile picture and IP address with us.

The following information is added to Firebase:

  • your vocab sets and kanji lists
  • your choices: picked sets, practice direction and mode, colors and light or dark
  • a practice you quit midway, including what you'd written for the question on screen, so Resume can bring it back
  • your practice history, if you turn on data collection in History

You can delete your account at any time in Settings. Open Settings and choose Delete my account.

Deleting your data

You can delete the data you generated from the site at any time.

  • Delete guest data: open Settings → Delete local storage.
  • Delete your account: open Settings → Delete my account.
  • Practice history: the History page deletes single sessions, or all of them at once.

Note that Firebase may keep a deleted sign-in record in its backups for up to 180 days before removing it.

Other services the site uses

  • GitHub: GitHub Pages.
  • Firebase: Firebase privacy.
  • Google's sign-in: Google's privacy policy.

The site sets no cookies as of October 2026; it uses your browser’s storage only to keep your data and sign-in working.
