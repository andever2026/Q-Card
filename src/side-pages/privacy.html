Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to https://andever2026.github.io/Q-Card/.
Repo on GitHub: https://github.com/andever2026/Q-Card.

Your privacy is important. Here’s the short summary of our policy:

  • When using this site as a guest, no data is stored in our database. It would be the same as if a user downloaded the page and used it offline.
    This may change if we add advertisements in the future.
  • When you sign in with a Google account, we store the Google account name, email address, profile picture, IP address and some browser details. We also store your practice sets and some preferences in our Firebase database.
    More information may be stored to our database as required when enabling additional features, but those are optional and we will ask for permission before storing.
  • All personal information that we collect fulfils a purpose. Consenting to permissions works incrementally and is reversible.
  • You can delete your data and your account at any time in Settings.

Last updated: October 25, 2026.

Using the site as a guest

To keep everything functional locally, all your information and data is stored in your browser's local storage.

This covers:

  • your vocab sets and kanji lists
  • your preferences and choices: picked sets, practice direction and mode, site colors, and light or dark mode
  • a practice you quit midway, including what you had written for the question on screen
  • your practice history, if you turn on data collection in History

Be careful when clearing cookies and website data. It’s recommended to back up your data often (Settings → Export). Your data is irrecoverable when lost.

You can delete this data at any time in Settings. Open Settings and choose Delete local storage.

Sign in with Google

By signing in with Google, you confirm that you are 13 or older.

Signing in for the first time creates a profile of you in Firebase. This includes sharing your name, email address, profile picture and IP address with us.

The following information is stored in Firebase:

  • your vocab sets and kanji lists
  • your preferences and choices: picked sets, practice direction and mode, site colors, and light or dark mode
  • a practice you quit midway, including what you had written for the question on screen
  • your practice history, if you turn on data collection in History

You can delete your account at any time in Settings. Open Settings and choose Delete my account.

Deleting your data

You can delete the data you generated from the site at any time.

  • Delete guest data: open Settings → Delete local storage.
  • Delete your account: open Settings → Delete my account.
  • Practice history: from the History page, delete single sessions or all of them at once.

Note that Firebase may keep a deleted sign-in record in its backups for up to 180 days before removing it.

Other services the site uses

  • GitHub: GitHub Pages.
  • Firebase: Firebase privacy.
  • Google's sign-in: Google's privacy policy.

The site uses no cookies as of October 2026; it uses your browser’s storage only to keep your data and sign-in working.
