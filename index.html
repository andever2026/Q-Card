Japanese Vocab Practice

This tool aims to improve written Japanese skills by combining traditional Q-Cards with handwriting recognition.

To start, sign in with Google to sync your data across devices, or continue as a guest to keep it in this browser only.

Help train a better handwriting checker?

The checker that reads your writing learns best from real handwriting like yours. If you say yes, each answer you write from now on is saved to your account with:

  • the strokes you drew: their shape, timing and pen pressure
  • the word you were answering, and how the checker and you marked it
  • whether you wrote with a pen, a finger or a mouse

Only the site's owner uses it, to tune and train the checker. It's never sold or shown to anyone else. You can turn it off or delete what you've shared any time in Settings. Only say yes if you're 16 or older.

Export / import

Export saves every vocab set and kanji list, plus the collection order and courses, as one file. Import reads a file like that: sets and lists already here are replaced by the file's version, new ones are added, and nothing is deleted. Export first if you want a copy to go back to.
